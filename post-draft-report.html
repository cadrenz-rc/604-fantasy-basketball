604 FANTASY BASKETBALL · SPECIAL REPORT

Strategist begins the chase for a three-peat

2026–27 Post-Draft Report · League desk

The draft is complete. Fourteen teams. Ten rounds. One defining question: can Strategist win a third consecutive championship?

The official Yahoo draft results confirm a 14-team, 10-round snake format, with Strategist drafting from No. 11 and GWA₱o closing the opening round at No. 14. The champion opened with Anthony Edwards, followed by Tyrese Haliburton, Anthony Davis and Jaren Jackson Jr. through four rounds. Alex Sarr, VJ Edgecombe, Norman Powell, Keegan Murray, Ayo Dosunmu and DeMar DeRozan complete a flexible, multi-category title-defense roster.

Early power structure

Coach Carter’s Bench Players leaves draft night with the strongest overall profile: Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Jalen Brunson, Bam Adebayo, OG Anunoby, Rudy Gobert and Josh Hart form a balanced, difficult-to-attack core.

LA Pampanga could be the most dangerous head-to-head categories opponent. Jalen Johnson, Jamal Murray, Jalen Williams, Donovan Clingan, Ausar Thompson, Naz Reid, Immanuel Quickley and Jakob Poeltl offer category depth.

TribuWakwak might own the league’s highest ceiling: Victor Wembanyama leads a group featuring Trae Young, Stephen Curry, Desmond Bane, Brandon Miller, Tyler Herro and Zion Williamson.

Strategist is fourth in the preseason rankings—but carries the league’s defining legacy storyline.

The late-slot winners

Money Back Guaranteed drafted Tyrese Maxey at No. 13, then paired him with Karl-Anthony Towns, Jaylen Brown, Pascal Siakam, Jarrett Allen, Brandin Podziemski and Christian Braun.

GWA₱o made the most of the No. 14 turn with Donovan Mitchell and Austin Reaves, adding Kyrie Irving, Deni Avdija, Kel’el Ware, Paul George, Andrew Wiggins, Tari Eason and Yves Missi. The late first round did not prevent either team—or Strategist—from assembling a serious contender.

The specialists

Adobar91 features Giannis Antetokounmpo, LaMelo Ball, Jalen Duren, Dyson Daniels, Michael Porter Jr., De’Aaron Fox and Miles Bridges. It looks like a force in rebounding and field-goal efficiency, with free throws and three-point percentage potential vulnerabilities.

Jthrust started at No. 1 with Nikola Jokić, then selected Josh Giddey, Trey Murphy III, LeBron James, Stephon Castle, Aaron Gordon and Bennedict Mathurin. Playmaking and rebounding are the calling cards.

Philly in 3 took a defense-first approach with Cade Cunningham, Amen Thompson, Walker Kessler, Dejounte Murray, Jalen Suggs, Toumani Camara and Myles Turner.

Boom-or-bust teams

Neil’s Swell Team may have the league’s widest range of outcomes. Kevin Durant, Joel Embiid, Kawhi Leonard, Ja Morant, CJ McCollum, Jerami Grant, Dylan Harper, AJ Dybantsa and Cason Wallace bring substantial upside and volatility.

Tree Planters should not be dismissed despite a No. 14 preseason ranking. Scottie Barnes, Devin Booker, Paolo Banchero, Julius Randle, Brandon Ingram, Yaxel Lendeborg and Nic Claxton provide genuine offensive upside.

Preseason power ranking

HEADLINE ENTERING WEEK 1

The target is on Strategist’s back.

Two championships have already been secured. The challengers are better positioned, the category builds are sharper, and the field is deeper. Now a champion drafted at No. 11 must survive thirteen rivals to complete the rare three-peat.
