604 FANTASY BASKETBALL · WEEK 1 · REPORTER'S DESK

Opening Week: The Three-Peat Chase Begins

The draft is over. Seven head-to-head matchups now put preseason expectations to the test. These editorial picks reflect draft-night rosters and category construction—not live projections or confirmed lineups.

FEATURED · #4 VS #13

StrategistvsLakay xt16n

The defending two-time champion starts with Anthony Edwards, Tyrese Haliburton, Anthony Davis and Jaren Jackson Jr. Lakay counters with Jayson Tatum, Evan Mobley, Domantas Sabonis and Darius Garland.

Reporter’s edge: Strategist. The champion has broader category paths, but Lakay's bigs can turn rebounds and FG% into a fight. Player availability is a major swing factor.

#7 VS #12 · VOLATILITY WATCH

#WEMB-VPvsNeil’s Swell Team

Cooper Flagg, Alperen Şengün, James Harden and Onyeka Okongwu face Kevin Durant, Joel Embiid, Kawhi Leonard and Ja Morant.

Reporter’s edge: #WEMB-VP. Neil has explosive upside, but health and availability risk makes this the hardest matchup to forecast.

#8 VS #5

Adobar91vsMoney Back Guaranteed

Giannis Antetokounmpo, LaMelo Ball, Jalen Duren and Dyson Daniels meet Tyrese Maxey, Karl-Anthony Towns, Jaylen Brown and Pascal Siakam.

Reporter’s edge: Money Back Guaranteed. Adobar91 can pressure boards and defensive categories; Money Back has the cleaner FT% and shooting profile.

#9 VS #2

JthrustvsLA Pampanga

Nikola Jokić and Josh Giddey lead Jthrust's playmaking attack against Jalen Johnson, Jalen Williams, Jamal Murray, Donovan Clingan and Ausar Thompson.

Reporter’s edge: LA Pampanga. Jokić can carry assists and rebounds, but LA Pampanga's depth and defensive production are difficult to match.

#10 VS #14 · UPSET WATCH

gamefaceonvsTree Planters

Luka Dončić, Chet Holmgren, Derrick White and Franz Wagner face Scottie Barnes, Devin Booker, Paolo Banchero and Julius Randle.

Reporter’s edge: gamefaceon. More balanced blocks and playmaking give them the nod. Tree Planters, listed as Tree Planters in the draft report, can surprise with offensive volume.

#6 VS #3 · CONTENDER CLASH

GWA₱ovsTribuWakwak

Donovan Mitchell, Austin Reaves, Kyrie Irving and Deni Avdija challenge Victor Wembanyama, Stephen Curry, Trae Young, Desmond Bane and Zion Williamson.

Reporter’s edge: TribuWakwak. Wembanyama's block ceiling and elite perimeter talent stand out, but GWA₱o can fight for points and assists.

#11 VS #1 · TOP SEED TEST

Philly in 3vsCoach Carter’s Bench Players

Cade Cunningham, Amen Thompson, Walker Kessler and Myles Turner face Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Jalen Brunson, Bam Adebayo, OG Anunoby and Rudy Gobert.

Reporter’s edge: Coach Carter. The preseason favourite has few obvious weaknesses, although Philly's defensive specialists can make steals and blocks competitive.
