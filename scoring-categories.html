The 9 Scoring Categories

Each weekly matchup compares two fantasy teams across these nine categories. Winning more categories wins the matchup. These are category results, not a single total fantasy-points score.

FG%

Field Goal Percentage

Field goals made ÷ field goals attempted. A higher percentage wins. Shooting volume affects the team-wide percentage.

FT%

Free Throw Percentage

Free throws made ÷ free throws attempted. Higher wins. Missed free throws affect the team-wide percentage.

3PT%

Three-Point Percentage

Three-point field goals made ÷ three-point attempts. Higher wins. This is percentage, not total made threes.

PTS

Points

Total points scored by active fantasy starters during the scoring week. Higher wins.

REB

Rebounds

Total offensive and defensive rebounds. Higher wins.

AST

Assists

Total assists credited to active fantasy starters. Higher wins.

STL

Steals

Total steals. Higher wins.

BLK

Blocks

Total blocked shots. Higher wins.

TO

Turnovers

Total turnovers committed. Lower wins—the only category where less is better.

How a 9-category matchup works

Each category contributes one win, loss, or tie to the weekly matchup. For example, 5–4 means a GM won five of the nine categories. The site's results reflect the commissioner-provided Yahoo matchup scores.

Only the Yahoo-selected active lineup contributes to a fantasy matchup; bench production does not count.
